## 3. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

### 3.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: name, email address, password (hashed), phone number, country of residence, date of registration
- **Profile Information**: professional title, industry, target job preferences, salary expectations, language preferences, profile photo (optional)
- **CV / Resume Content**: work experience, education, skills, certifications, languages, publications, references, professional summary, and any other information you choose to include in your CV
- **Cover Letter Content**: personal messages, narratives, and any text generated or edited by you
- **Communications**: messages you send through your Jobvero email alias (`yourname@getjobvero.com`) to recruiters or any other party
- **Interview Coach Data**: voice recordings (if you use the voice interview feature), transcriptions, AI-generated feedback
- **Payment Information**: billing name, billing address, last 4 digits of payment card, payment history (full payment details are processed by Stripe and never stored on our servers)

### 3.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data**: pages visited, features used, clicks, search queries, time spent on the Service
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, language settings, time zone
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: see Section 9 below
- **Email Engagement**: when emails sent on your behalf are delivered, opened, clicked, or bounced (via Resend webhooks)

### 3.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Job Board APIs**: job listings retrieved from Adzuna, JSearch, Reed, France Travail, and other partners (these are public job listings, not your personal data)
- **Authentication Providers**: if you sign up using Google, Apple, or another OAuth provider (if implemented), we receive your name, email, and profile picture from that provider

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have certain rights regarding your personal data. We respect these rights regardless of where you live and grant equivalent rights to all our Users.

### 7.1 Rights Under GDPR (EEA/UK Users)

You have the right to:

- **Access**: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification**: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure ("Right to be forgotten")**: request deletion of your personal data
- **Restriction**: request limitation of the processing of your data
- **Data portability**: receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
- **Objection**: object to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent**: where processing is based on consent, withdraw your consent at any time
- **Lodge a complaint**: with your local Data Protection Authority (e.g., CNIL in France, AEPD in Spain, CNPD in Portugal, BfDI in Germany, ICO in the UK)

### 7.2 Rights Under CCPA/CPRA (California Users)

California residents have the right to:

- **Know** what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
- **Delete** personal information collected from them (subject to certain exceptions)
- **Opt out** of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising)
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information
- **Limit the use** of sensitive personal information
- **Non-discrimination** for exercising these rights

### 7.3 Rights Under LGPD (Brazilian Users)

Brazilian residents have rights equivalent to those described above, including the right to access, correct, anonymize, port, or delete their personal data.

### 7.4 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at **support@getjobvero.com** with the subject line "Privacy Request" and specify which right you wish to exercise. We will respond within thirty (30) days (or sooner where required by applicable law). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.
